The Problem

Working Conditions

Heat stress is a workplace hazard. High temperatures affect worker safety, productivity and health.

Heat Accumulation

Poor ventilation in industrial facilities causes heat buildup in upper areas and plant rooms, reducing equipment efficiency.

Operational Impact

Sustained heat above 25°C reduces output, increases errors, and accelerates equipment degradation.
